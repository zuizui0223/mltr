\noindent\textbf{Scope of this supplement.}
The main article frames the work as an ecological decision-sufficiency audit. This supplement records the finite definitions, specialized proof details, implementation, reproducibility checks, and additional examples needed to reproduce that audit. Coarsest partition refinement, source-to-target state-abstraction transfer, targeted monitoring, value-of-information analysis, and history-sensitive equivalence are established prior literatures and are not claimed here as generic inventions.
