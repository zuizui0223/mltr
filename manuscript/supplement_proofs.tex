\section{Specialized proof details}

This section records finite proof details separately from the ecological narrative in the main text. Where a statement is a direct specialization of established partition-refinement machinery, we state the prior-art role explicitly and also give the finite specialization needed here.

\subsection{Operational decision-sufficiency criterion}
\begin{proof}[Proof of the operational portability theorem]
Necessity follows because an exact carried state interface must factor the target output, legal-action row, and every declared successor through its carried label. Hence each of these quantities is constant inside a carried fiber.

For sufficiency, assume those quantities are constant in every carried fiber. Define the quotient output and legal-action row by their common values on each fiber. For each legal action, successor carried labels are also constant, so the quotient successor is well defined. Therefore all declared target observations and legal futures factor through the carried classification, which is exact for the declared interface.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Local obstruction}
\begin{proof}[Proof of the local-obstruction proposition]
If the inherited interface is not exact, at least one factorization condition in the preceding proof is not well defined. Thus there exist two target states in one inherited fiber that differ in current output, legal-action row, or the carried label of a successor under a declared legal action. Repeating the same argument along a shortest distinguishing legal future gives a finite witness. Conversely, any such pair contradicts exactness of the carried classification.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Source-relative exact repair: exact logical dependency}
The main manuscript cites Paige--Tarjan (1987) and Givan--Dean--Greig (2003) to establish that coarsest refinement from an arbitrary supplied partition is established prior art. The manuscript invokes only that generic \emph{type} of construction. Its correctness in the present deterministic exact-interface setting does not depend on an unproved external theorem, because the specialization is proved below.

For a classification $P$, define the refinement signature of state $x$ by
\[
\sigma_P(x)=
\Bigl(P(x),y(x),\bigl(\ell_x(a),s^P_x(a)\bigr)_{a\in A}\Bigr),
\]
where $\ell_x(a)=1$ if $a\in L(x)$ and $0$ otherwise, and
\[
s^P_x(a)=
\begin{cases}
P(\delta(x,a)), & a\in L(x),\\
\bot, & a\notin L(x).
\end{cases}
\]
Let $T(P)$ be the partition induced by equality of $\sigma_P$, and initialize
\[
P_0=C,\qquad P_{k+1}=T(P_k).
\]

\begin{proposition}[Finite source-relative exact repair]
The sequence $(P_k)$ stabilizes after finitely many nontrivial refinements at a partition $P_\ast$. The fixed point $P_\ast$ is exact, refines $C$, and is the unique coarsest exact target partition refining $C$. Hence $P_\ast=Q^\ast$.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Because $P(x)$ is included in $\sigma_P(x)$, $T(P)$ can split blocks of $P$ but cannot merge them. Every nontrivial update therefore strictly increases the number of blocks. Since $X$ is finite, the sequence stabilizes after at most $|X|-|C|$ nontrivial updates.

At a fixed point $P_\ast=T(P_\ast)$, two states in the same $P_\ast$ block have identical signatures. They therefore have identical outputs, identical legal-action sets, and, for each legal action, successors in the same $P_\ast$ block. This is exactly the target exactness condition, so $P_\ast$ is exact.

It remains to prove coarseness. Let $E$ be any exact target partition satisfying $E\preceq C$. We show inductively that $E\preceq P_k$ for every $k$. The base case is $E\preceq C=P_0$. Suppose $E\preceq P_k$ and $E(x)=E(x')$. Exactness of $E$ gives
\[
y(x)=y(x'),\qquad L(x)=L(x'),
\]
and, for every legal $a$,
\[
E(\delta(x,a))=E(\delta(x',a)).
\]
Because $E\preceq P_k$, equality of successor $E$ labels implies equality of successor $P_k$ labels. The induction hypothesis also gives $P_k(x)=P_k(x')$. Hence
\[
\sigma_{P_k}(x)=\sigma_{P_k}(x'),
\]
so $P_{k+1}(x)=P_{k+1}(x')$. Therefore $E\preceq P_{k+1}$.

By induction, every admissible exact $E$ refines the fixed point $P_\ast$. Thus $P_\ast$ is coarser than every exact refinement of $C$. Such a greatest element is unique, proving $P_\ast=Q^\ast$.
\end{proof}

The references above are therefore citations of prior-art status and general algorithmic context, not hidden proof dependencies.

\subsection{Minimum conditional label information}
Let $X_\mu\sim\mu$ and write $C=C(X_\mu)$, $Q=Q^\ast(X_\mu)$, and $P=P(X_\mu)$.

\begin{proposition}[Minimum conditional repair information]
For every exact target classification $P$ refining $C$,
\[
H_\mu(Q\mid C)\le H_\mu(P\mid C).
\]
If carried class $c$ contains $k_c$ repaired $Q^\ast$ blocks, then
\[
0\le H_\mu(Q\mid C)
\le \sum_c \Pr_\mu(C=c)\log_2 k_c
\le \log_2\max_c k_c.
\]
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
By the preceding proposition, every exact refinement $P$ of $C$ also refines $Q^\ast$. Therefore there is a deterministic map $f$ such that $Q=f(P)$. Consequently
\[
H_\mu(P\mid C)
=H_\mu(Q,P\mid C)
=H_\mu(Q\mid C)+H_\mu(P\mid Q,C)
\ge H_\mu(Q\mid C),
\]
which proves the minimum-information statement.

Conditional on $C=c$, the random variable $Q$ has at most $k_c$ possible values, so
\[
H_\mu(Q\mid C=c)\le \log_2 k_c.
\]
Averaging over $c$ gives
\[
H_\mu(Q\mid C)
=\sum_c \Pr_\mu(C=c)H_\mu(Q\mid C=c)
\le \sum_c \Pr_\mu(C=c)\log_2 k_c
\le \log_2\max_c k_c.
\]
Nonnegativity of conditional entropy gives the lower bound.
\end{proof}

This proposition is an information-theoretic corollary of coarsest exact repair. It is not used to claim a new general entropy theorem.

\subsection{Exact monitoring realization and minimum measurement cost}
For $S\subseteq J$, recall that $z_S(x)=(z_j(x))_{j\in S}$ and that $S$ realizes $Q^\ast$ relative to $C$ when a decoder $d_S$ satisfies
\[
Q^\ast(x)=d_S(C(x),z_S(x))
\]
for every target state.

\begin{proposition}[Monitoring realization criterion]
A selected measurement set $S$ realizes $Q^\ast$ relative to $C$ if and only if every obstruction pair $(x,x')\in E$ is separated by at least one selected measurement:
\[
\forall (x,x')\in E\;\exists j\in S:
\quad z_j(x)\ne z_j(x').
\]
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
For necessity, suppose $S$ realizes $Q^\ast$ and take $(x,x')\in E$. If no selected measurement separated the pair, then
\[
C(x)=C(x')
\quad\text{and}\quad
z_S(x)=z_S(x').
\]
The decoder would receive identical inputs and return the same repaired label, contradicting $Q^\ast(x)\ne Q^\ast(x')$.

For sufficiency, assume every pair in $E$ is separated by at least one selected measurement. Consider two states with
\[
C(x)=C(x')
\quad\text{and}\quad
z_S(x)=z_S(x').
\]
If their repaired labels differed, $(x,x')$ would belong to $E$, but then some selected measurement would separate them, a contradiction. Therefore $Q^\ast(x)=Q^\ast(x')$ whenever the inherited label and selected-measurement vector agree.

Define $d_S$ on every realized tuple $(C(x),z_S(x))$ by
\[
d_S(C(x),z_S(x))=Q^\ast(x).
\]
The preceding argument makes this definition independent of the representative state. Extend $d_S$ arbitrarily to unrealized tuples. Then $d_S$ reconstructs $Q^\ast$ for every target state, so $S$ realizes the repair.
\end{proof}

For each measurement $j$, define
\[
A_j=\{(x,x')\in E:z_j(x)\ne z_j(x')\}.
\]

\begin{corollary}[Minimum-cost monitoring realization]
The least-cost exact monitoring realization over the candidate library is
\[
\min_{S\subseteq J}\sum_{j\in S}c_j
\quad\text{subject to}\quad
\bigcup_{j\in S}A_j=E.
\]
A realization exists if and only if
\[
\bigcup_{j\in J}A_j=E.
\]
Any obstruction pair outside this union is an explicit certificate that the supplied measurement library cannot recover the repaired classification exactly.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
By the monitoring realization criterion, a selected set is feasible exactly when its measurements collectively separate every obstruction pair, which is the set-cover condition. Minimizing additive acquisition cost over feasible sets gives weighted set cover. Feasibility of at least one selected set is equivalent to coverage by the full candidate library.
\end{proof}

This is the central bridge from the structural audit to monitoring design. It states exactly what a candidate field-observation library must distinguish; it does not claim a new generic set-cover algorithm and it does not address measurement error.

\subsection{Structural defect does not determine decision regret}
\begin{proposition}[No universal defect--regret ordering]
For every integer $d\ge1$ and every real $r\ge0$, there exists a finite target operational system, inherited classification $C$, coarsest exact repair $Q^\ast$, target-state distribution $\mu$, finite management action set, and payoff function such that
\[
|Q^\ast|-|C|=d
\qquad\text{and}\qquad
\mathcal{R}_\mu(C,Q^\ast;u)=r.
\]
Consequently structural state-count defect alone provides neither a nontrivial universal upper bound nor a positive universal lower bound on decision regret, and it cannot universally rank management consequence.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Fix $d\ge1$ and let
\[
X=\{x_0,x_1,\ldots,x_d\}.
\]
Let $C$ put all states in one inherited class. Equip the target operational system with one legal self-loop action at every state and distinct declared outputs
\[
y(x_k)=k.
\]
Because exact classes must have constant output, every exact refinement of $C$ must separate all $d+1$ states. Hence the coarsest exact repair $Q^\ast$ is the singleton partition and
\[
|Q^\ast|-|C|=(d+1)-1=d.
\]

Let the target distribution satisfy
\[
\mu(x_0)=\mu(x_1)=\tfrac12,
\qquad
\mu(x_k)=0\quad(k\ge2),
\]
and let the downstream management action set be $U=\{a_0,a_1\}$. Define payoffs by
\[
 u(x_0,a_0)=2r,\qquad u(x_0,a_1)=0,
\]
\[
 u(x_1,a_0)=0,\qquad u(x_1,a_1)=2r,
\]
with zero payoffs on the remaining zero-probability states.

Using $Q^\ast$, the decision rule chooses $a_0$ at $x_0$ and $a_1$ at $x_1$, so
\[
V_\mu(Q^\ast;u)=2r.
\]
Using $C$, both positive-probability states share one label and must receive the same action. Either action yields expected value $r$, so
\[
V_\mu(C;u)=r.
\]
Therefore
\[
\mathcal{R}_\mu(C,Q^\ast;u)=r.
\]
Because $r$ was arbitrary, the same structural defect can coexist with zero, arbitrarily small, or arbitrarily large regret. Choosing two different defects and reversing their choices of $r$ directly violates any universal monotone ranking by state-count defect.
\end{proof}

This negative result concerns information structure versus decision consequence. It is compatible with value-of-information theory: once probabilities, utilities, and measurement costs are specified, those quantities should be analyzed directly rather than inferred from partition size.

\subsection{Route coherence and carried-map context}
\begin{proof}[Proof of route coherence]
If all root-to-terminal routes induce the same complete carried terminal map, the terminal system receives one route-independent inherited classification. Since exact refinement is a deterministic function of the terminal system and that supplied classification, the repaired interface is route independent as well. If two complete carried maps differ, at least one terminal configuration receives incompatible inherited labels, so no single route-free carried classification represents both declared routes. This second statement is an obstruction on the carried labels only. It does not assert that the unlabeled repaired classifications obtained from the two routes must differ after refinement, and the theorem is therefore stated as a sufficient condition for route independence rather than as an equivalence.
\end{proof}

\begin{proof}[Proof of the carried-map context statement]
Group declared routes by equality of their complete carried terminal maps. One immutable context for each equivalence class is sufficient because the inherited map is well defined within every context and exact refinement can then be applied. It is necessary because routes with different complete maps assign incompatible inherited meanings to at least one terminal configuration and therefore cannot share one immutable carried-label context. Thus one context per distinct carried terminal map is the minimum for this declared route representation.
\end{proof}

The final argument is a scoped closure statement for the declared replacement graph. It does not claim that retaining history, path identity, or causal context is a new idea in formal systems generally.
