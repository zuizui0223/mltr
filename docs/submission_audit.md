# MLTR submission-readiness audit

## Publication-facing identity

- **Repository:** MLTR — Macro-Law Transport and Repair.
- **Recommended article title:** *Do Ecological Macro-Laws Survive Structural Replacement? Route Coherence and Minimal Historical Completion*.
- **One-sentence thesis:** An inherited ecological state is reusable only when its source meaning can be carried consistently and remains sufficient for target outputs and actions; carriage composes along a fixed route, while genuinely different routes require historical context exactly when their complete carried terminal maps differ.

`EXT` is retained only as the historical development name and the compatibility package name `ext_transport`; it is not the public article identity.

## Claim hierarchy for the manuscript

| Level | Formal/ecological object | Role in paper | Required wording boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| I | carried-label consistency under a many-to-many replacement relation | source-semantic foundation | declared relation; target inheritance is undefined if one target state receives different source labels |
| II | direct = sequential carried semantics | formal supporting result | relational composition itself is standard; claim only the source-label carriage contract under total declared relations |
| III | route coherence + minimum history completion | headline formal package | different routes share one history-free meaning iff complete carried terminal maps agree; do not claim generic path dependence as new |
| IV | operational decision sufficiency | headline ecological interpretation | inherited states must agree on target outputs, feasible actions, and action-conditioned successor meanings |
| V | local obstruction + exact refinement | established infrastructure used diagnostically | initial-partition coarsest refinement, finite termination, and uniqueness are prior machinery; ecological value is the missing decision distinction returned by the split |
| VI | transport defect + accumulating family | supporting diagnostic witness | block-count/bit increase is not standalone mathematical novelty or an empirical scaling law |

The manuscript must not present all levels as equal discoveries. Levels I--IV carry the source-relative Paper A story. Levels V--VI make the audit executable and interpretable but must be explicitly credited to established refinement machinery and diagnostic definitions.

## Required formal proof package

Before submission, the manuscript—not the repository alone—must contain proofs or explicit prior-art delegation for the following statements under one consistent notation:

1. local criterion for when a declared many-to-many relation induces one carried source-label map;
2. direct/sequential carried-label equivalence for consecutive total relations;
3. operational decision-sufficiency criterion on a well-defined carried target partition;
4. finite local target obstruction;
5. coarsest relative exact refinement, explicitly identified as established constrained partition-refinement machinery;
6. route independence under equality of complete carried terminal maps; and
7. minimum history completion by equality classes of those maps.

Finite replay artifacts are regression evidence for supplied witnesses. They are not automated proofs of all-finite-system claims.

## Prior-art firewall

The manuscript must explicitly acknowledge that the following patterns are established:

- coarsest stable refinement of a supplied initial partition;
- fixed-point existence/termination/uniqueness in finite refinement;
- local split witnesses for unstable blocks;
- user-specified initial distinctions in model reduction;
- generic ecological path dependence and historical contingency.

Use Fernandez (1990), Ranzato & Tapparo (2007), Paige & Tarjan, and Argyris et al. (2023) as formal anchors. The Paper A contribution must not be defended by saying merely that the initial partition came from the source.

## Ecological decision-sufficiency gate

A reader should be able to state the framework without formal-methods terminology:

> An old ecological state label remains usable after change only if sites with that label still imply the same relevant observations, available interventions, and intervention responses. If not, the failure identifies what additional ecological distinction should be monitored. History is recorded only when different declared replacement routes change that inherited operational meaning.

If the abstract, cover letter, or Discussion cannot express this idea directly, the manuscript is not ready for Theoretical Ecology.

## Scope language to preserve verbatim in spirit

- The state spaces, outputs, action grammar, and replacement relations are declared finite-model objects.
- The theory does not infer the source--target relation or replacement history from field data.
- A positive transport defect does not prove that no smaller target-only abstraction exists after abandoning source provenance.
- Path incoherence does not prove that all ecological models must store history.
- Exact refinement is a benchmark after inherited semantics are fixed, not a claim that partition refinement is new.
- The present theory does not yet cover stochastic, approximate, continuous, recurrent, or partially observed replacement systems.

## Relationship to CCOC

CCOC asks how independently optimized exact interfaces change when future grammars are opened or composed. MLTR instead fixes one accepted source meaning and asks whether it can be carried, reused, and interpreted consistently after non-nested structural replacement. A newly legal target action can be an MLTR audit input because it exposes decision insufficiency inside one inherited state, but CCOC's cross-grammar minimum-interface lower bound is not an MLTR theorem.
